## Project Overview

<div style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #667eea 0%, #764ba2 100%); color: white; padding: 2em; border-radius: 15px; margin: 2em 0;">
  <h3 style="color: white; margin-top: 0;">Research Objective</h3>
  <p style="margin-bottom: 0; font-size: 1.1em; line-height: 1.6;">
    This project aims to uncover the gene expression landscape of the pig jejunum through single-cell RNA sequencing. By analyzing and visualizing the data, we explore the diversity of cell types and their biological functions within the jejunal tissue, providing crucial insights into gut immunity and metabolism.
  </p>
</div>

<div style="background-color: #f8f9fa; border-left: 4px solid #3498db; padding: 2em; border-radius: 0 10px 10px 0; margin: 2em 0;">
  <h3 style="color: #2c3e50; margin-top: 0;">Scientific Impact</h3>
  <p style="color: #2c3e50; margin-bottom: 1em;">
Using single-cell RNA-seq data, we have created a comprehensive map of the cell populations within the jejunum, providing valuable information for further research into gut-related disorders and diseases in pigs.
  </p>
  <p style="color: #2c3e50; margin-bottom: 0;">
    This research contributes to a deeper understanding of the role of the intestine in health and disease, with potential applications in veterinary medicine and agricultural practices.
  </p>
</div>

## Key Features & Methodology

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(300px, 1fr)); gap: 2em; margin: 2em 0;">

<div style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #ff9a9e 0%, #fecfef 100%); padding: 2em; border-radius: 15px; box-shadow: 0 4px 15px rgba(0,0,0,0.1);">
  <h4 style="color: #2c3e50; margin-top: 0;">Data Source</h4>
  <p style="color: #2c3e50; margin-bottom: 0;">
    High-quality single-cell RNA sequencing data from pig jejunum tissue, providing unprecedented resolution of cellular heterogeneity in intestinal biology.
  </p>
</div>

<div style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #a8edea 0%, #fed6e3 100%); padding: 2em; border-radius: 15px; box-shadow: 0 4px 15px rgba(0,0,0,0.1);">
  <h4 style="color: #2c3e50; margin-top: 0;">Analysis Tools</h4>
  <p style="color: #2c3e50; margin-bottom: 0;">
    Utilizes cutting-edge single-cell RNA analysis tools including <strong>Seurat</strong> and <strong>Scanpy</strong> for comprehensive data processing and analysis.
  </p>
</div>

<div style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #ffd89b 0%, #19547b 100%); color: white; padding: 2em; border-radius: 15px; box-shadow: 0 4px 15px rgba(0,0,0,0.1);">

  <p style="color: white; margin-bottom: 0;">

## Technical Highlights

<div style="background-color: #e8f5e8; border: 2px solid #27ae60; padding: 2em; border-radius: 10px; margin: 2em 0;">
  <h3 style="color: #27ae60; margin-top: 0;">Computational Approach</h3>
  <ul style="color: #2c3e50; font-size: 1.05em;">
    <li><strong>Quality Control:</strong> Rigorous filtering and preprocessing of single-cell data</li>
    <li><strong>Dimensionality Reduction:</strong> PCA, UMAP, and t-SNE for data visualization</li>
    <li><strong>Clustering Analysis:</strong> Identification of distinct cell populations</li>
    <li><strong>Differential Expression:</strong> Discovery of cell-type-specific gene signatures</li>
    <li><strong>Functional Annotation:</strong> GO and pathway enrichment analysis</li>
  </ul>
</div>
